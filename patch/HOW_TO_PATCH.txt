HOW TO MAKE THE SUPER RYU WORLD 1 ROM
=====================================

This game needs a patched ROM. You make it yourself from your own copy of
Super Mario World and the official patch in this folder.

What you need
-------------
Super Mario World (USA) - the original 1990 SNES release, unmodified.
  Headerless .sfc: 524,288 bytes (512 KB)
  CRC32  B19ED489
  SHA-1  6b47bb75d16514b6a476aa0c73a683a2a4c18765
A headered .smc (524,800 bytes) is fine for "Patch My ROM.bat" (it removes
the header). The Japanese, European and Super Mario All-Stars versions do
NOT work.

EASY WAY (Windows)
------------------
1. Drag your Super Mario World (USA) ROM onto "Patch My ROM.bat"
   (or double-click it and pick the ROM).
2. It checks the ROM, applies the patch with Floating IPS and saves
   "Super Ryu World 1.sfc" in this folder (CRC32 D6A6317E).
3. Open the game's exe, click Browse ROM in the first-run setup and pick
   "Super Ryu World 1.sfc".

Manual way
----------
Floating IPS: run flips\flips.exe, click Apply Patch, pick
  "Super Ryu World 1.bps", pick your clean (headerless) ROM, save as "Super Ryu World 1.sfc".
Online: https://www.marcrobledo.com/RomPatcher.js/ - ROM file = your clean
  ROM, Patch file = "Super Ryu World 1.bps", Apply patch.
The result must have CRC32 D6A6317E.
